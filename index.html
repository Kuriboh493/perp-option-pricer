What the model assumes

  1. Perp fair value. Funding accrues continuously at κ(F − S) + ιS, with κ = 1 / funding interval. No arbitrage then gives F = S(κ − ι)/(κ − (r − q)).
  2. Perp drift. Because the perp tracks spot, its risk-neutral forward is F·e^((r−q)T). It is not a martingale like a dated future, so Black-76 with the mark as forward is slightly off.
  3. Diffusion. Spot is lognormal with constant volatility between jumps. No stochastic volatility, so the smile comes only from jumps.
  4. Jumps. Merton's model: Poisson arrivals, lognormal sizes, drift compensated so the forward is unchanged. Jump risk is priced as if diversifiable.
  5. Market hours. Variance accrues per hour, and a closed hour carries a fixed fraction of an open hour's. The input volatility is calibrated so a full week matches its calendar-time value. Holidays are ignored.
  6. Everlasting options. Daily-style funding of mark minus payoff makes the price Σ 2^(−i) · C(i·Δ), a weighted basket of European options one funding period apart.
  7. Settlement. Linear USD-margined perp, premium paid up front in USD, European exercise against the perp mark, no fees, no liquidation or oracle risk, flat rates.
